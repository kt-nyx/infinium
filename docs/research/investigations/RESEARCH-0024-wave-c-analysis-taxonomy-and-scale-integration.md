## 3. Accepted taxonomy

The normative product result is
[Skyrim SE mod-impact taxonomy version 0.1.0](../../product/mod-impact-taxonomy.md).
It has five independent axes:

1. declared purpose and intended target;
2. technical modification surface;
3. affected game system or content area;
4. consequence type; and
5. faceted effect extent.

It is versioned, hierarchical where useful, multi-label, and open to
evidence-backed extension. Unknown, unsupported, unmapped, and not-applicable
are distinct applicability states. Severity, confidence, symptoms, evidence
authority, analyzer maturity, readiness, review state, and installation
topology remain separate.

The taxonomy dependency map and its product, architecture, and evaluation
consumers were updated during acceptance. Acceptance does not imply complete
taxonomy coverage or analyzer support.

## 5. Performance and evaluation limits

RESEARCH-0022's retained artifacts support the accepted logical
candidate-selection direction for their author-coupled synthetic scope. They
do not pass EVAL-0032 or establish real-mod recall, finding accuracy, causal
case grouping, persistence behavior, or production capacity.

RESEARCH-0023 establishes a measurement method and rough local feasibility
bounds only. Its RQ-027-specific measurement history is not a durable,
replayable production benchmark. Exact scan-time, memory, IO, disk, checkpoint,
and LLM-cost budgets must be measured after authoritative adapters and
candidate architecture prototypes exist.
